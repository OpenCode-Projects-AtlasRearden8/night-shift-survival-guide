Step 3 — The pre-shift checklist

Run this in about five minutes, most days:

  • Work clothes laid out
  • Food bag packed (see meals chapter)
  • Thermos / water bottle filled
  • Phone charging outside the bedroom
  • Nap in progress or completed
  • Household knows: "window starts at ______"
  • DND / call-twice rule set on phone
  • Earplugs + eye mask within arm's reach of the bed
  • Sunglasses by the door for the morning drive
  • Badge, keys, and lunch money where you left them
  • Work alarm / ride confirmed
  • Blackout curtains pulled (daytime sleep rule #1)

Step 4 — The drive-home card (copy into your car)

  • Sleepiness signs memorized: eye heaviness, lane wander, missed signs, micro-nods, lost miles
  • Nap-before-driving planned when needed (15–30 min, alarm on)
  • Coffee nap option: coffee, then 20-min nap while caffeine ramps up
  • Bailout spots known on route: ______ , ______ , ______
  • Backup ride number: ______
  • Sunglasses in the car
  • Someone knows my usual arrival window: ______
  • Phone fully charged before leaving work

Step 5 — Review (once per cycle, 5 minutes)

Three questions, answered honestly:

  1. Which sleep window did I protect fully this stretch? ______ of ______ days
  2. What broke it? (one line: e.g., "after-late upstairs neighbor", "kid sick", "5 a.m. coffee trap") ______
  3. One change for the next cycle (exactly one): ______

That's the entire improvement loop. One change per cycle, assessed at next review — the same way you'd adjust anything else on the unit.
