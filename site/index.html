Night Shift Survival Guide

A warm, plain-English field manual for nurses and other night workers.

Plain English, one small step at a time, never condescending. Built for nurses, paramedics, and everyone else who lives real life while the sun is on the wrong side of the sky.

First time here? Start with Start here, then skim sleep-window planning and fill in the shift-cycle planner worksheet. One small change per week beats a full overhaul you abandon by Friday.
